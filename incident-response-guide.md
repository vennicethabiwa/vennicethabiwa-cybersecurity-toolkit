# Incident Response Guide for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in Gaborone, Botswana

## Purpose
This guide tells small businesses what to do when they are hacked or data is stolen (NIST, 2024).

## Step 1 - Do Not Panic, Disconnect Immediately (NIST, 2024)
- Disconnect the affected computer from Wireless Fidelity (Wi-Fi) and internet immediately
- Do NOT turn off the computer, keep evidence for Information Technology (IT) support
- Inform your manager

## Step 2 - Identify What Happened
- What Personally Identifiable Information (PII) was affected? For example, customer names, Omang numbers, bank details (Government of Botswana, 2018)
- When did it happen and who reported it?

## Step 3 - Contain and Report
- Change all passwords from a clean computer and enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) (Google, 2024)
- If money was lost, contact your bank and report to Botswana Police Service Cybercrime Unit
- If customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII) was leaked, you must report as per Botswana Data Protection Act (Government of Botswana, 2018)

## Step 4 - Recover and Learn (NIST, 2024; BOCRA, 2023)
- Restore files from backup that was stored on external hard drive
- Scan all devices with antivirus
- Train staff again on phishing awareness (BOCRA, 2023)
- Update your security checklist
