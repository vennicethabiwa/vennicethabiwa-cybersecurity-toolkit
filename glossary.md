# Glossary - Full Forms and Definitions for Short Forms Used in Toolkit

This glossary explains all short forms (abbreviations) used in this toolkit (BOCRA, 2023; NIST, 2024).

| Short Form | Full Form | Meaning in Simple Words |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| SME | Small and Medium Enterprise | Small business in Botswana |
| MFA | Multi-Factor Authentication | Password + code from phone, two checks for safety (Google, 2024) |
| BOCRA | Botswana Communications Regulatory Authority | Regulator for communications and cybersecurity in Botswana |
| CISA | Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency | USA agency that gives phishing advice (CISA, 2024) |
| NIST | National Institute of Standards and Technology | USA institute that made small business security framework (NIST, 2024) |
| USB | Universal Serial Bus | Small flash drive / memory stick |
| WiFi | Wireless Fidelity | Wireless internet without cable |
| SMS | Short Message Service | Text message on phone |
| URL | Uniform Resource Locator | Website address link, e.g. www.bocra.org.bw |
| PII | Personally Identifiable Information | Customer private data like Omang, phone, bank details (Government of Botswana, 2018) |
| IT | Information Technology | Person who fixes computers |
| FNB | First National Bank | Bank in Botswana |

All definitions are based on BOCRA (2023), NIST (2024) and CISA (2024).
